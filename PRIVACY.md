# TabZolo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

TabZolo does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data.

## What TabZolo stores

When you turn focus mode on, TabZolo saves a list of your open windows and tabs (their URLs, titles, order, pinned state and window position) so it can reopen them when you turn focus mode off. It also saves whether focus mode is on or off.

This is stored only in your browser, using Chrome's local extension storage. It never leaves your device. TabZolo makes no network requests, has no analytics or tracking, and uses no remote code.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling TabZolo deletes everything it stored.
